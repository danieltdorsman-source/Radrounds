    These cases teach recognition and radiology-centred next steps. Card images are real normal/reference CT frames where available; they are not presented as the actual pathology case unless explicitly stated. The pathology is taught in the findings and decision framework.

    Stroke lab

    NCCT → CTA → escalation.

    A junior-doctor workflow for acute stroke imaging: establish haemorrhage status, look for early ischaemic change and mimics, define the vessel question, then communicate the result and timing clearly. Local stroke pathways and specialist advice take precedence.

    On rounds

    Make the request answerable.

    The useful request is not “pain ?cause”. It tells radiology what happened, what you found, the important safety context, and the precise clinical question the scan needs to answer.

    Do not enter patient identifiers into this educational tool. Use your clinical system for real patient details.

    Why RadRounds labels some images “reference”

    It is better to show a real normal/reference image and explicitly teach the abnormal pattern in text than to pass off a generated pseudo-scan as genuine pathology. When an image is a reference rather than the pathology case, the site says so.
